# FinnHEMS base at Helsinki-Vantaa (EFHK) for FlightGear, lit with compositor lights

The FinnHEMS helicopter base at Helsinki-Vantaa airport as a FlightGear
scenery object. Its lights are real light sources for
FlightGear's HDR compositor: they light the hangar, the apron and whatever
is parked on it, at night and at dusk. It was made to go with the
[UH-60A](https://github.com/MrChillStorm/UH-60), as a scenery that shows
what compositor lights can do.

## What's there

- **The building:** the hangar and office wing, 44 m long, 17.3 m deep and
  7.3 m high, placed from OpenStreetMap's outline. Along its front: the
  hangar's blue fabric door (open by default, so you can see into the lit
  hangar), the lettering, a canopy over the entrance and the glazed ground
  floor of the offices.
- **The apron** in front of it.
- **27 lights:**
  - six high-bays in the hangar,
  - four floodlights under the roof edge, lighting the apron,
  - three downlights under the canopy,
  - five uplights on the lettering,
  - nine ceiling panels in the offices.

  Each fixture's lens glows too. The lights come on as the sun sets (from
  4° above the horizon to 2° below) and are off by day.

## Requirements

FlightGear 2024.2 or a later nightly, with the HDR compositor:
`--compositor=Compositor/HDR/hdr`. Other renderers show the building
without its lights.

## Installing

Clone or download this repository and add it to your scenery path, for
example:

```bash
fgfs --fg-scenery=/path/to/EFHK-FinnHEMS --airport=EFHK
```

To start on the apron, facing the building, at night:

```bash
fgfs --fg-scenery=/path/to/EFHK-FinnHEMS --compositor=Compositor/HDR/hdr --lat=60.316931 --lon=24.982889 --heading=71 --timeofday=midnight
```

## Switches

Set them from the property browser, telnet or a Nasal console:

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| `/scenery/finnhems/door-closed` | 1 closes the hangar door |
| `/scenery/finnhems/dim` | dims every light, from 0 (full) to 1 (off) |
| `/scenery/finnhems/lights-off` | 1 switches every light off |

FlightGear's lights cast no shadows, so the floodlights also light a parked
helicopter's cockpit through its roof. Dim them if that bothers you.

## Rebuilding

The model and its textures are generated by `tools/build.py` (Python 3 with
Pillow):

```bash
python3 tools/build.py
```

It writes everything in `Objects/e020n60/e024n60/`. `tools/preview.py`
draws a quick flat-shaded view of the model, without FlightGear.
